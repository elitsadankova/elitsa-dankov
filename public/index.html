Good to know

Do I need a patch test?

Yes — a quick patch test is required at least 48 hours before your first tint or lift, and again if it's been more than 6 months since your last visit.

How should I prepare?

Come with clean lashes and brows — no makeup, mascara or oils. Avoid brow waxing or tweezing for a week before lamination.

Aftercare

Keep lashes and brows dry and avoid steam, saunas, makeup and skincare on the area for 24 hours. After that, brush brows daily and use a nourishing serum to keep the hairs healthy.

Is it suitable during pregnancy?

Hormones can affect results, so please mention it when booking and we'll decide together.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule so the slot can be offered to someone else.
